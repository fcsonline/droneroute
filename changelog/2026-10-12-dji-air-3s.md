## Summary

Add a dedicated DJI Air 3S export profile for DJI Fly, including facade scans
with explicit camera pitch and aircraft heading commands.

## Changes

- Add DJI Air 3S alongside all existing models, including M30, M30T, and M30 Dock.
  Use a separate profile for the DJI Fly archive layout, namespace, and aircraft
  metadata, preserving existing saved missions and exports.
- Keep facade headings and camera pitches when importing old M3E missions.
- Preserve fractional heights, camera action parameters, and Air 3S segment
  action boundaries on import.
- Set Air 3S missions to use the takeoff point as the 0 m reference by default.
  Keep waypoint height numbers unchanged and configure takeoff clearance in DJI
  Fly.
- Add reference-based regression tests and document supported actions and
  remaining controller/flight validation.
